# Design decisions for apps 11–15

Keep the portfolio’s cool white and navy surfaces, teal #087f75, blue #316caa, and violet #7255a0 accents. DM Sans carries controls and body copy, Amiri carries Arabic, and Newsreader gives Parables Studio a slower editorial opening. Fonts remain local. Both themes preserve the same hierarchy and use visible focus indicators.

Each interface has a specific job. The Places Atlas opens with actual regional land geometry and a numbered evidence legend. Concept Connections spends its visual emphasis on sixteen large term tiles, followed by four revealed groups. The history challenge uses a vertical sequence with explicit move buttons. Salah Lab centers a source reading and a hide/reveal meaning control. Parables Studio uses three selectable diagram nodes and keeps commentary before private reflection.

The map uses public-domain Natural Earth land polygons with an equirectangular regional projection. No political borders are drawn. Five city/heritage contexts have cited UNGEGN/UNESCO coordinates. Other entries stay in the reading list without speculative map pins. A text legend provides the same links as the SVG markers.

Prayer Arabic and Quran Arabic have visibly different source labels. Audio is native, local, optional, and never autoplays. Al-Fatiha lessons explicitly play the entire surah. Original English prayer meanings and approximate Roman guides are labeled accordingly.

Games keep recovery optional and count attempts or hints without claiming religious merit. All ordering works with buttons rather than requiring dragging. Diagrams use HTML buttons with pressed states and retain source text. Reflections stay in memory, and users explicitly copy or download them. Small screens use one-column reading layouts while the sixteen-term game keeps a four-by-four board with generous tile heights.
