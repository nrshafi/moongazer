# Family Halaqa

## Overview

Ten-minute Islamic activities to do together. Portfolio rank 19 of 20, independent project `apps/family-halaqa`. Published with static Cloudflare Pages hosting at https://family-halaqa.moongazer.tech/.

## Goals and visitor flow

Select one of twelve packs by topic, suggested age or search. Read a source, try the three-option reading check and retry if needed. Discuss the open prompt, try a shared activity or choose to pass. Optionally remember the sitting, print all prompts and the facilitator’s answer, or share completion without participant details.

Keep the complete activity usable immediately without an account, personal history, paid inference or a live API. All runtime content/fonts and interactions are local. Explicit bookmarks and temporary recovery are optional.

## Features and content

- `packs`: 12 records.

There are 20 exact source-list entries, 16 generated HTML pages, source/privacy/404 routes, print controls, both themes and a clear-data dialog. The app has its own manifest, lockfile, fonts and license files.

## Scope and limits

Twelve packs contain 36 activities in total: a reading check, open discussion and shared activity per pack. Suggested age bands are 5–7, 8–11 and 12+, with 10–15 minute editorial estimates. Pickthall’s historical wording may need an adult’s explanation. Open discussions have no single graded answer and anyone may pass.

Twelve single-sitting family packs combine a source reading question, an open discussion and a shared activity. Age bands are editorial suggestions and Pickthall’s historical English may need an adult’s explanation. Open prompts have no single graded answer. Activities are original applications beyond the fetched canonical text, not scholarly rulings from quran.ai, quran.com or quran.foundation. No child names, private stories, profiles or ongoing behavior records are collected. Every participant may pass.

Accounts, databases, user identity, external runtime assets, synchronized multiplayer and automatic publication are outside this implementation.
