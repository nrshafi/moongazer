# Halal Business Decisions

## Overview

Work through fictional business dilemmas, then inspect the choices and sources. Portfolio rank 17 of 20, independent project `apps/halal-business-decisions`. Implemented and packaged for static Cloudflare Pages hosting; no account publication has occurred.

## Goals and visitor flow

Choose a fictional business dilemma. Read the known facts, make one of three first decisions and one of two follow-up decisions, and inspect each consequence in the ledger. Return to the full Quran reading and named reports. Restart or copy a citation-rich summary.

Keep the complete activity usable immediately without an account, personal history, paid inference or a live API. All runtime content/fonts and interactions are local. Explicit bookmarks and temporary recovery are optional.

## Features and content

- `cases`: 12 records.

There are 27 exact source-list entries, 16 generated HTML pages, source/privacy/404 routes, print controls, both themes and a clear-data dialog. The app has its own manifest, lockfile, fonts and license files.

## Scope and limits

Twelve cases have six complete paths each, 72 paths total. Choices and consequences are fictional applied reasoning, not transaction certification, predicted rewards, personalized financial advice or a scholarly ruling from quran.ai, quran.com or quran.foundation. Full passages, original paraphrases and modern applications remain distinct.

Twelve fictional two-stage dilemmas connect a source reading with an explicitly editorial modern application. Outcomes are written possibilities, not promised worldly results or judgments of a person. Canonical Quran text and Pickthall wording come from Quran MCP; Al-Muyassar provides concise explanation and Al-Qurtubi is linked for further legal discussion where fetched. Hadith notes are original paraphrases of specifically identified reports. The exercises do not certify a real transaction as halal, replace a qualified adviser or supply legal/financial advice.

Accounts, databases, user identity, external runtime assets, synchronized multiplayer and automatic publication are outside this implementation.

## Success criteria

Core flows work with empty or blocked storage. Activity logic, source integrity and route links pass verification. Both themes fit desktop and mobile widths with keyboard/native controls. `bun run check`, `bun test` and `bun run build` succeed independently. The release archive contains static files at its root.
