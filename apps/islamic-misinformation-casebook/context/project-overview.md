# Islamic Misinformation Casebook

## Overview

Inspect a claim, open the evidence, and decide what the sources support. Portfolio rank 20 of 20, independent project `apps/islamic-misinformation-casebook`. Published with static Cloudflare Pages hosting at https://islamic-misinformation-casebook.moongazer.tech/.

## Goals and visitor flow

Choose a fixed claim exercise. Read the precise claim and source reading, open two evidence cards, select a bounded conclusion and reveal the correction with its limit. Retry, save the case, print the evidence or copy a correction that keeps the citations and limit attached.

Keep the complete activity usable immediately without an account, personal history, paid inference or a live API. All runtime content/fonts and interactions are local. Explicit bookmarks and temporary recovery are optional.

## Features and content

- `cases`: 12 records.

There are 22 exact source-list entries, 16 generated HTML pages, source/privacy/404 routes, print controls, both themes and a clear-data dialog. The app has its own manifest, lockfile, fonts and license files.

## Scope and limits

Twelve constructed reading claims are fixed evidence exercises, not measured viral trends or accusations against real people. The three findings are contradicted by the cited text, needs context, and beyond the cited evidence. Omission from one report does not prove absence from every other source. Sharing preserves this limit.

Twelve constructed reading claims are checked against fixed, specifically identified sources. They are exercises in misquotation, context, sequence and evidence limits, not a list of measured viral trends or accusations about people. Classifications describe what the cited material supports. “Beyond the cited evidence” does not mean that every other source has been searched or that the broader proposition is false. The app accepts no arbitrary claim for live judgment. Quran readings are canonical, and commentary and hadith notes remain attributed original paraphrases.

Accounts, databases, user identity, external runtime assets, synchronized multiplayer and automatic publication are outside this implementation.
