# Islamic Civilization Timeline

## Overview

Explore connected histories across regions, with dates and evidence in view. Portfolio rank 16 of 20, independent project `apps/islamic-civilization-timeline`. Published with static Cloudflare Pages hosting at https://islamic-civilization-timeline.moongazer.tech/.

## Goals and visitor flow

Browse chronological records using topic, region, search and inclusive date-band overlap filters. Compare two records and copy the comparison link; open an event to see its precision note and institutional source. Save bookmarks or copy a saved reading list.

Keep the complete activity usable immediately without an account, personal history, paid inference or a live API. All runtime content/fonts and interactions are local. Explicit bookmarks and temporary recovery are optional.

## Features and content

- `events`: 24 records.
- `landPaths`: 153 records.

There are 20 exact source-list entries, 28 generated HTML pages, source/privacy/404 routes, print controls, both themes and a clear-data dialog. The app has its own manifest, lockfile, fonts and license files.

## Scope and limits

Twenty-four records span 661 to 1648 CE across nine regions. Century ranges are inclusive editorial filtering bands, not exact event durations. Overlap is not proof of contact, influence or causation. The map gives orientation without borders or exact event markers. No Quran text is used to support a later historical date.

Twenty-four selected records from UNESCO and the Metropolitan Museum of Art cover political changes, learning, cities, trade and architecture from 661 to 1648 CE. Century bands use editorial numeric bounds for filtering; exact years and construction ranges retain the institution’s precision. The map uses public-domain Natural Earth land shapes with no political borders. It is regional orientation, not a map of historical sovereignty. No Quran passage is used to prove a later historical date.

Accounts, databases, user identity, external runtime assets, synchronized multiplayer and automatic publication are outside this implementation.
