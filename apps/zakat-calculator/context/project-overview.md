# Zakat Calculator

## Overview

Work out your zakat step by step, with the method shown. The planned audience is a learner or family member who wants to see how each asset, debt and nisab basis enters a zakat calculation, and where scholars differ, which choice produces which result.

Portfolio app for the P7 Ramadan season. Project root `apps/zakat-calculator`, port 4341, planned production host `https://zakat-calculator.moongazer.tech/`. The calculator, method page and zakat al-fitr page are built (P7 T7.6). Not yet deployed or listed in the directory.

## Current state

- Home: the calculator. Step 1 assets (currency label default BDT, cash, business stock, money owed to you, debts due now, gold and silver by weight, worn jewellery, metal known by value), step 2 gold and silver price per gram plus the nisab weight conversion (a choice), step 3 four labelled differences (jewellery, debts due, receivables, nisab metal). No option is selected by default; Calculate asks for any unanswered choice and moves focus to the first.
- Result: at or above the nisab (text), zakat due (1/40 of the zakatable total), nisab used, line-by-line breakdown, the choices made with their attributions, the limit sentence, print button, end-of-session block (share text has no digits).
- `/method/`: every entry of the verified method record with Quran text copied from the record, hadith as reference, grading and paraphrase with a sunnah.com link, attributed positions, notes and a plain "what we could not open" section (AAOIFI SS 35 and others).
- `/zakat-al-fitr/`: entries `fitr-facts` and `fitr-food-vs-cash`, no amounts, with "Ask your local masjid or zakat committee for this year's amount."
- Sources & methodology: lists every distinct source of the record (generated by script).
- Content: `src/data/content.json` holds `{"method": <method.json>}`, copied by `scripts/sync-method.ts`; `provenance.json` records the sha256 of the method record, the verification record and the content file. A test checks content.json equals the record. The record is a second-pass source check, not a scholarly review.

## Features (T7.6, built)

- Inputs for cash and bank balances, gold and silver (by weight or value), business stock at market value, money owed to the visitor that they expect to receive, and short-term debts due now. Currency is a free label, with no conversion.
- Nisab from prices the visitor enters per gram for gold and silver. The app does not fetch prices.
- A labelled choice for each school difference in the verified method record, each with a "Learn more" link to the method page.
- A result showing whether the total reaches nisab, the zakat due on the zakatable total, a line-by-line breakdown, the choices made and a printable summary, with the limit sentence "This shows the calculation for the choices you make. It is not a fatwa; ask a scholar you trust about your situation."
- Zakat al-fitr explainer, separate from the calculator.

Every difference shown maps to a method-record entry with at least one source (tested).

## Scope

In scope: the introduction, the sources page, the verified method once it exists, and the calculator built to the D23 rule. Out of scope: rulings or fatwas, personal advice, a currency converter, live prices, accounts, server storage and any claim of scholarly review.

## Success criteria

1. The home page and sources page are accurate about the current state, with no unverified claim.
2. `bun run check`, `bun test` and `bun run build` pass.
3. Once the calculator exists: pure tests cover the worked examples from the method record, and every difference shown has a verified source.
