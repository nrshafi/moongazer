Branch `phase-7-season`, verified 2026-11-12 (Asia/Dhaka). The agent released it under D2b.

## Changes

- **Zakat Calculator (T7.4–T7.6, new app):**
  - Lives at `zakat-calculator.moongazer.tech`, port 4341, accent blue, with 44 tests.
  - Works through assets, debts and the visitor's own gold and silver prices, step by step.
  - Wherever schools differ, the visitor picks a labelled option with its source note (D23). No ruling is preselected. Both nisab bases are shown, and the printed summary lists the choices made.
  - `/method/` explains each step with its citation. `/zakat-al-fitr/` shows who pays and when, and the documented difference between food and cash value.
  - The limit sentence ("not a fatwa") sits beside every result. There are no live prices, runtime APIs or third-party scripts.
- **Registration (T7.7):**
  - Added to `deployment/targets.json`, the SEO catalog and the learning guides, with a logo, a directory screenshot and a social card.
  - Added to the server allowlist for the anonymous counter.
  - The directory copy now reads "seventeen apps".
  - `_implementation/verify.ts` now runs every app under `apps/`, like CI.
  - `verify-static.py` allows the eight institutional fatwa domains cited by the method record.
  - The plan's `/zakat` short link was not added, because D2c rules out redirects and short links.
- **Ramadan dates (T7.9):**
  - The record is `_implementation/research/ramadan/dates.json`, from the Umm al-Qura calendar (ummulqura.org.sa).
  - Ramadan 1448 is expected to begin on 8 February 2027.
  - The last ten nights begin at sunset on 27 February.
  - Eid al-Fitr is 9 or 10 March, a range because UK moon-sighting predictions give 10 March.
  - The page labels these dates "Expected dates; confirm with your local moon sighting."
- **Ramadan hub (T7.10), `moongazer.tech/ramadan/`:**
  - It has its own title, description, canonical, breadcrumb and sitemap entry.
  - There are 30 night cards. Each card has:
    - a short ayah (exact Arabic and Pickthall's translation, drawn from the verified ayah-of-the-night list);
    - that night's Family Halaqa pack;
    - a Guess the Surah link. For 28 nights the link is the night's Juz round (`?mode=juz&juz=N`). Juz 2 and 5 contain no surah start, so those nights link to the daily puzzle instead.
  - The last-ten-nights section shows Surah al-Qadr in full and three paraphrased hadith with collection and number (Bukhari 2017, 2024, 2014).
  - It links to the Zakat Calculator and the zakat al-fitr page.
  - The calendar is printable, there is no countdown, and the page works without JavaScript.
  - The plan's Escape Room "Ramadan nights" rooms do not exist yet (C4 not started), so the cards carry no room link.
- **Guess the Surah:**
  - `?mode=juz&juz=N` opens the Juz tab on that juz, and the value is validated.
  - A juz with no surah start shows "No surahs here".
- **Seasonal slot (T7.11):** the directory shows a hidden Ramadan card. `homepage/app.js` reveals it between the dates in `homepage/seasonal.json`, 2027-01-25 to 2027-03-13.
- **Family Halaqa (C16):**
  - 30 "Ramadan nights" packs, one per night, spread across the age bands 5–7, 8–11 and 12+.
  - Each pack has a reading question, a discussion, a shared activity and a closing thought.
  - The facilitator's answer stays in a closed `<details>`.
  - New tests check:
    - that no title points at its answer, using word stems, related-word groups and a list of rejected titles;
    - that every prophet named carries an honorific.
