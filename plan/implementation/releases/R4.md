# R4 — Feedback, corrections log and anonymous counter (phase 4)

Branch `phase-4-services`, verified 2026-11-12T00:05+06:00 (Asia/Dhaka). Status: **released by the agent under D2b** (full delegation, 2026-11-11). Go-live results are recorded in the "Production" section below.

## Changes

- **Feedback form in every app.** A closed "Report an error or suggest something" panel sits above each app footer. It is a native form POST to `https://moongazer.tech/api/feedback` and works without JavaScript. It has three categories, a message of 10–2,000 characters and an optional email. Spam protection is a hidden honeypot field plus a minimum 3 s time-to-submit. Result panels carry a compact "Report an error" link that opens the form (`#site-feedback`).
- **Directory pages.** `/feedback/` has the same form. `/feedback/thanks/` (noindex, outside the sitemap) links back to the app the visitor came from, for known app names only. `/corrections/` is a public, dated corrections log seeded with the 14 R1 fixes. The footer and the About page link to both.
- **Anonymous counter.** `track()` sends `{app, item, event}` with `navigator.sendBeacon` to `https://moongazer.tech/api/event` for `activity_started`, `activity_completed` and `share_clicked`. It sends nothing off moongazer.tech, with Global Privacy Control, or without `sendBeacon`, and it has no cookie, identifier or visitor text. 16 apps are wired. Hifz Planner is wired when `phase-7-hifz` merges (its hook points are marked `// P4.7:`).
- **Backend.**
  - Pages Functions `/api/feedback` and `/api/event` run on the apex project, with D1 database `moongazer` (binding `DB`, migration `0001_init.sql`).
  - Feedback rows are stored as `pending`. Event counts are daily aggregates per app, item and event.
  - No IP address is stored.
  - Pure validation lives in `functions/_lib/validate.ts`, with 51 tests.
- **Owner scripts.** `deployment/scripts/review-feedback.mjs` lists and sets feedback, and `publish` writes a public correction without contact details. `counter-report.mjs` produces CSV totals and completion rates.
- **Privacy pages.** "Anonymous activity counts" and "Feedback you send" sections were added to every app. The CSP allows `connect-src` and `form-action` to `https://moongazer.tech` only.
- **CI.** Apps still deploy by Direct Upload (`--apps-only`). The apex now deploys with `wrangler pages deploy` from `homepage/`, so the Functions and the D1 binding ship with it. Validation runs `bun test homepage/tests`.
- **Fixes found during verification.**
  - The shared form first used `id="feedback"`, which clashed with many apps' own status lines. It is now `#site-feedback`.
  - Astro had dropped the whitespace between form parts, so screen readers ran words together. Explicit gaps were added, and the text-join audit is back to 0.

## Privacy wording (agent-approved under D2b)

The owner delegated this approval on 2026-11-11 ("i give you full permission. no need to take my input"). The approved text in `privacy.astro`:

> **Anonymous activity counts.** When you start, finish or share an activity, the page sends an anonymous count to moongazer.tech: the date, the app, the activity id and the event. No cookie, no IP address and no device details are stored, and nothing identifies you. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, no count is sent.
>
> **Feedback you send.** If you use "Report an error or suggest something", we store your message, its category, the page address and the date. An email address is stored only if you choose to give one, and it is never published. Messages are read before anything is changed; corrections appear in the public corrections log without your details.

The owner can revise this wording at any time. A change only needs `privacy.astro` edited in one app and synced.
