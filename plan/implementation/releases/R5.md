# R5 — Activity-first shell, design system and directory front door (phase 5)

Branch `phase-5-shell`, verified 2026-11-12 ~14:30 (Asia/Dhaka). Released by the agent under D2b.

## Changes

- **Design system:**
  - The root `DESIGN.md` defines families and tokens with computed contrast (all pairs ≥ 4.5:1; `_implementation/contrast.py`), typography, spacing, motion, the five archetypes with phone wireframes, shared components and an accessibility checklist.
  - `homepage/DESIGN.md` covers the directory.
  - Each of the 16 apps has its own `DESIGN.md` with its measured first-control top.
- **Family colours:** Quran is teal, Seerah and history gold, worship and daily life blue, family and group violet. Small text uses `--accent-ink` and text on accent fills uses `--on-accent`. Gold is new.
- **Shared shell:**
  - A compact hero (H1 = app name, one-line descriptor, at most 140 px on phones).
  - A header with logo and name at every width, plus an "All apps" link.
  - The star watermark is removed.
  - The learning guide is now an accordion.
  - Arabic is set at 1.6–1.8 rem with line-height ≥ 1.9.
  - Print CSS, an opt-in projector mode, and a correct-answer pulse (none under reduced motion).
  - JetBrains Mono (local, OFL) for metadata chips in the Playboard and Dossier apps.
- **New shared components:**
  - `HowItWorks.astro`: the drawer that now holds every caveat that sat above an activity.
  - `session-end.ts`: result, then what you learned, then Copy and WhatsApp share (never the answer), then the next app, then "Report an error". It has tests.
- **Every app:**
  - Its activity is on screen one, with a drawer, an end-of-session block and its own motif.
  - Print and projector where its DESIGN.md says so.
  - Ayah Threads share links no longer reveal the theme.
  - Seerah Timeline's phone inspector is a bottom drawer.
  - The Hifz Planner wizard from P7 is merged (juz and surah choosers, counter events), with its first control moved from 665 px to 416 px.
  - Understand Your Salah's lessons now come before the audio card.
- **Directory:**
  - A compact hero, a "Tonight on Moongazer" card, and quick-picker chips that work without JS (new `time`, `audience` and `format` fields in `targets.json`).
  - The library with real app screenshots, compact subject guides, a seasonal slot and a trust row.
  - ﷺ renders in Amiri (a 1.6 KB subset).
  - The build fails if any local file a page references is missing.
- **Logos:** all regenerated under the new slugs in family colours. Family Halaqa's mark had drawn human figures; it is now an open book on a rug under a crescent. Social cards show the new hosts. Both shipped earlier as a hotfix.
- **No short links:** dropped under D2c.
